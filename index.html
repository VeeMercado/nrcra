NRCRA.ORG.AU Website prototypes

Three directions for the five-page NRCRA website (Home, About, Partners, Network, R.A.C.K), each drawn from a different part of the mood board, plus a full-width image experiment (1.2). Sample content only. To be built in Wix Classic. Status: draft for direction review.

Prototype 1

Colour block sections

Inspired by Milou's drafts and current sites strong colour blocks. Rounded cards in light teal, yellow and orange tints, a navy hero panel beside a big rounded photo, pill buttons.

  • Colour cards with photos inside
  • Five pillars as arched columns
  • Partner section: photo, story and logos
