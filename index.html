Three directions for the five-page website (Home, About, Partners, Network, R.A.C.K), each drawn from a different part of the mood board. The client chose Prototype 1.

Prototype 1

Colour block sections

Inspired by Milou's drafts and current sites strong colour blocks. Rounded cards in light teal, yellow and orange tints, a navy hero panel beside a big rounded photo, pill buttons.

  • Five pillars as arched columns
  • Partner section: photo, story and logos

Light and fresh

Inspired by the R.A.C.K app and Resilient Lismore. Airy white and pastel bands with photos front and centre. Soft rounded photo shapes, small logo-arc accents, one illustration and softly rounded buttons.

  • Five pillars as photo-topped columns
  • River-style journey timeline
